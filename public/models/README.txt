============================================================
3D MODELS DIRECTORY
============================================================

Place your unrigged 3D model (.glb or .gltf) here.
Example:
  public/models/character.glb
  public/models/prop.glb
  public/models/diorama.glb

Recommended Export Settings from Blender:
- Format: glTF Binary (.glb)
- Geometry: Apply Modifiers, Include Normals
- Textures: Embedded or 1K/2K resolution
- Compression: Draco (optional, for ultra-fast download)
============================================================
